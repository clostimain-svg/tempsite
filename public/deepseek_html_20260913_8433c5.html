Shipping Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Shipping Destinations

We currently ship to all addresses within the United States.

2. Processing Time

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking information once your order has shipped.

3. Shipping Methods & Delivery Time

  • Standard Shipping: 7-15 business days — Free on orders over $35
  • Expedited Shipping: 3-7 business days — $9.99

4. Shipping Costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over $35. For orders under $35, a flat rate of $4.99 applies.

5. Tracking

All orders include tracking. You will receive a tracking number via email within 24 hours of shipment.

6. Contact

For shipping inquiries, contact us at support@yourbrand.com.
