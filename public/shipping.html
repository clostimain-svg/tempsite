Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Refund Eligibility

You may request a refund within 30 days of receiving your order. To be eligible, the item must be unused and in the same condition as received, in its original packaging.

2. How to Request a Refund

Contact us at support@yourbrand.com with your order number and reason for return. We will provide return instructions within 48 hours.

3. Refund Processing

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of approval. Approved refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 5-10 business days.

4. Non-Refundable Items

Gift cards and personal care items are non-refundable.

5. Damaged or Defective Items

If you received a damaged or defective item, please contact us immediately with photos. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no additional cost.

6. Contact

Email: support@yourbrand.com
